About

I am an Electrical Engineer and Technical Project Manager based in Denmark, with experience across electrical project delivery, HV/MV/LV systems, substations, critical power, industrial infrastructure, construction, commissioning and handover.

I currently work with Turner & Townsend as a Technical Project Manager – OFCI, supporting the delivery of owner-furnished equipment packages on a major data centre project in Denmark. My role brings together vendors, contractors, engineering, schedules, site readiness, quality and commissioning interfaces.

My background combines technical engineering with practical execution. I have worked across design coordination, supplier and contractor interfaces, equipment packages, FAT/SAT, site installation, QA/QC, commissioning readiness, technical close-out and handover.

Earlier roles included HV/MV substations, protection and control, SCADA/RTU interfaces, UPS-backed critical-power systems, industrial electrical delivery and power-systems research.
